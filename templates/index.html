What Makes Us Different

🟢

Live Oval Frame Monitor

OpenCV draws a real-time face-framing oval on your webcam feed. The glow ring shifts from green → amber → red based on detection status, so you always know if you're properly centred.

✦ New in v2
🧠

NLP Voice Processing

Filler words (um, uh, like…) are stripped in real-time. Keyword extraction surfaces the most relevant terms in your answer. Confidence scoring measures your speech pace, hesitation rate, and vocabulary richness.

✦ New in v2
🎙️

Voice Sentiment Analysis

Every answer is analysed for tone — positive, neutral, or negative — giving you feedback on how your language comes across to interviewers.

✦ New in v2
🤖

Claude-Powered Evaluation

Anthropic's Claude evaluates every answer across accuracy, depth, clarity, and relevance — returning structured scores with specific strengths and improvements.

🛡️

Anti-Cheat Proctoring

Face absence, multiple faces, gaze deviation, and tab-switching are all detected and logged. A full risk report is generated at the end.

📊

Detailed Report

Review scores by question, dimension breakdowns, keyword highlights, NLP confidence trends, and proctoring logs — all in one export-ready report.
